Rohan Reddy - In-Class 1 v3 Notes

Widget Tree:
Flutter screens are made from widgets nested inside other widgets.

Stateless vs. Stateful:
A stateless widget does not need to remember changing information.
A stateful widget can store changing information and rebuild when needed.

Controllers & Lifecycle:
initState() creates resources when the widget starts.
dispose() cleans up resources when the widget is removed.

Declarative UI:
Flutter describes what the screen should look like based on the current state.
setState() tells Flutter to rebuild the UI.
